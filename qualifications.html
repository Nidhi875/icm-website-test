Our Qualifications

At Gouldings Global Academy, we design qualifications that bridge the gap between academic study and real-world career success. Our programmes are taught entirely online and rigorously marked in the UK by experienced academics and industry practitioners, ensuring the highest standards of British educational excellence.

As part of our commitment to student success, we offer every student a 10-week Level 3 Award completely free as part of their course package. Available across a dynamic variety of topics, this foundational step equips learners with the practical, job-ready skills needed to secure part-time roles when they start university internationally. Through our partnership with the Institute of Commercial Management (ICM) and strategic industry collaborations, our qualifications deliver a competitive edge and are respected by top-tier institutions across the UK, Canada, Australia, and beyond.

Level Descriptor

Qualification levels define the knowledge, understanding, practical skills, responsibility and autonomy expected from learners at each stage of study.

As learners progress through the levels they develop increasingly advanced analytical, technical and professional competencies, preparing them for higher education and employment opportunities.

Titling of Gouldings Global Academy Qualifications

Following a comprehensive review of qualification titles, Gouldings Global Academy has adopted a clear and consistent qualification naming structure that aligns with internationally recognised academic standards.

Qualification titles are designed to help learners, employers, universities and regulatory organisations clearly identify the qualification level, qualification type and subject area.
